📜 BAL SABHA RULES

Please read and follow these rules during every Bal Sabha.
  1. 1Respect the speaker. No talking while the presenter is speaking.
  2. 2Raise your hand. Wait to be called on before speaking.
  3. 3Keep your hands and feet to yourself.
  4. 4Use inside voices only. No shouting or yelling.
  5. 5No eating. Water only.
  6. 6Respect your karyakars. Listen to them and follow their instructions.
  7. 7Be respectful to everyone. No teasing, bullying, or inappropriate language.
  8. 8Stay seated and pay attention during presentations.
  9. 9No running or playing around during Bal Sabha.
  10. 10Keep the room clean. Pick up after yourself.
  11. 11Participate with a positive attitude in all activities.
  12. 12Keep phones and other distractions away unless permitted.
  13. 13Be on time and ready for Bal Sabha.
  14. 14Respect the mandir. Behave appropriately in all sacred spaces.
  15. 15Follow BAPS values. Show humility, discipline, kindness, and respect.
⭐ These are the main rules!
